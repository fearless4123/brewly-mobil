# Brewly — Privacy Policy

Brewly is an educational project. User preferences and demo order history may be stored in the device's <code>localStorage</code>. The application is not designed to store personal data on an external server.

No external tracking cookies are used inside the Tauri WebView. Please do not enter real or sensitive personal data. This educational application is not a commercial data processing service and does not aim to collect real user data under the Turkish data protection law (KVKK).
